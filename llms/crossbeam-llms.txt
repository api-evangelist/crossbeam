## Overview
>Crossbeam turns your partner data into pipeline and revenue. Working together is easy. Sharing data isn’t. Crossbeam changes that with secure Account Mapping, a layer of intelligence, and automated workflows that turn your partner network into your most powerful growth engine. 
The future of GTM is AI-driven — and it’s only as powerful as the data and context your AI and teams can access. Crossbeam delivers proprietary, partner-sourced signals and intelligence you can’t find anywhere else. It’s your edge in the AI economy.
Crossbeam turns that ecosystem data into action — helping GTM teams prioritize the right accounts, engage the right partners, and prove what’s actually driving revenue.


It’s not just about managing partnerships. It’s about activating them — and turning every signal into something your GTM team can use. Powered by AI, built on data only your partners can provide.




Crossbeam is a secure data collaboration platform that allows companies to safely compare data with their partners and uncover shared customers, overlapping prospects, and opportunities for co-selling and co-marketing. By enabling these insights without exposing sensitive information, Crossbeam helps organizations identify where their business interests align and activate joint go-to-market efforts with confidence.

We call this Ecosystem Intelligence: transforming partnership data (shared customers, prospects, open opportunities, and more) into actionable insights that fuel AI—both within Crossbeam and across your broader tech stack. 
By integrating with tools across the tech stack, Crossbeam ensures that these insights flow seamlessly into existing sales, marketing, and partnership workflows.


At its core, Crossbeam integrates  directly with tools like CRMs and data warehouses, allowing users to upload their data into Crossbeam and define data segments known as ‘populations’ - such as customers, opportunities, or prospects, and automatically find overlaps with their partners’ data. These secure mappings reveal where teams can collaborate to drive pipeline, strengthen relationships, and accelerate revenue growth.


Security and trust are central to Crossbeam’s mission. The platform is built with enterprise-grade protections, including SOC 2 Type II compliance, GDPR alignment, data encryption at rest and in transit, and rigorous access controls. This strong security foundation allows organizations to collaborate on sensitive data safely and transparently, empowering them to scale partnerships without compromising privacy or compliance.

## Key Features
Crossbeam equips organizations with a powerful set of features designed to unlock the full potential of their partnerships by transforming shared data into actionable insights. Each feature is designed to power go-to-market motions—from identifying new prospects to closing deals faster—and to measure the true impact of the ecosystem.


Account Mapping lies at the heart of Crossbeam’s functionality. It allows companies to securely uncover where their accounts overlap with partners. These overlaps can reveal shared customers for retention efforts, mutual prospects for co-selling, and expansion opportunities within existing accounts. The mapping is automatic and secure, providing partnership, sales, and marketing teams with real-time visibility into their ecosystem landscape without ever exposing sensitive or unnecessary data.


Crossbeam Copilot delivers partner insights directly into the tools teams already use every day. Whether in Salesforce, HubSpot, Chrome, Gong, or Outreach, Copilot surfaces account-level partner context at the moment it’s most valuable (during sales conversations, prospecting, or pipeline review). By embedding Crossbeam intelligence natively in these platforms, Copilot ensures that ecosystem data becomes operational, helping sellers and marketers take immediate, data-driven action.


Deal Navigator prioritizes opportunities and prospects where partners can give you an edge. It provides intel only your partners can provide — like recommended contacts, buying signal, and tech stack details — to close with confidence. Sellers can instantly identify which partners can provide strategic insights, introductions, or competitive positioning to move deals forward. This feature helps prioritize high-value opportunities, improve win rates, and align go-to-market efforts between ecosystem partners. 


With Greenfield, users can identify accounts that exist in their partners’ CRMs but not in their own.
 This feature transforms the traditional discovery process by allowing organizations to surface net-new prospects that are already partner customers. It’s an especially powerful feature for sales, marketing and business development teams looking to expand reach and target warm leads with higher conversion potential.


Co-selling Workflows and Slack Alerts provide quick access to deal insights and keep teams updated on new accounts, opportunities, and overlaps.. Sellers can connect with partners to share intelligence, request introductions, or align on joint plays - all through streamlined, repeatable workflows. Slack notifications keep users informed of new overlaps, partner activities, or deal updates as they happen, allowing teams to act quickly and stay synchronized. Together, these features eliminate friction in communication and turn partnership insights into immediate, coordinated action.


The Performance Dashboard offers a comprehensive view of your ecosystem’s impact—directly within your CRM. It highlights which partners are influencing deals, which team members are effectively using partner insights, and where missed opportunities are occurring. This analytics-driven visibility enables teams to optimize co-selling performance, refine partner strategies, and identify training or process gaps. By connecting these metrics directly to revenue outcomes, Crossbeam turns ecosystem collaboration into measurable business success.


Attribution ensures that partner-sourced and partner-influenced deals are both visible and measurable.. Track how partners contribute to revenue by attaching their influence to specific opportunities.. This transparency enables teams to measure the true impact of their ecosystem on pipeline generation, deal acceleration, and overall growth. Attribution data can be used to build trust with partners, demonstrate ROI, and justify partnership investments within internal reporting.


The Offline Partners feature lets you map accounts with partners who aren’t connected to Crossbeam by uploading a CSV or Google Sheet... This flexibility ensures that no valuable partnership insights are left untapped and allows companies to include distributors, resellers, or smaller partners who operate outside digital integrations.


Shared Lists bring collaboration to life by allowing users to create, manage, and act upon lists of accounts directly within Crossbeam. These lists can include overlapping customers, mutual prospects, or non-overlapping strategic targets. Teams can annotate, share, and track progress on these lists in real time, enabling coordinated action between partnership, sales, and marketing teams. It transforms what was once static spreadsheet collaboration into a dynamic, interactive process.

## Ecosystem-Led Growth (ELG) Framework
> Crossbeam powers Ecosystem-Led Growth (ELG), 
The old GTM playbooks aren’t working. Ecosystem-Led Growth (ELG) isn't just another buzzword—it's a game-changing strategy transforming how companies attract, convert, and grow customer relationships by leveraging their partner ecosystems.
. Instead of relying solely on direct sales or traditional marketing channels, ELG focuses on leveraging trusted partner relationships to attract, convert, and retain customers. The premise is simple yet transformative: companies grow faster and more efficiently when they collaborate with their ecosystem by sharing insights, co-selling opportunities, and creating value together.


Crossbeam is the technology engine that powersELG. By securely connecting and comparing data between partners, Crossbeam enables companies to identify shared customers, mutual prospects, and opportunities for joint engagement, all while maintaining strict data privacy and security standards. This allows companies to operationalize their ecosystems: marketing teams can target new prospects, sales teams can uncover unique insights on their prospects, and partnership teams can measure the impact of their ecosystem on pipeline and revenue.


At its core, Ecosystem-Led Growth represents a shift in how GTM currently worksCompanies don’t operate alone—they thrive within ecosystems of partners and complementary solutions.. Crossbeam facilitates this collaboration through its network, where multiple organizations securely share data to uncover co-selling opportunities, cross-promotional plays, and expansion pathways. This interconnected approach drives mutual success, strengthens customer value propositions, and builds resilience through community-driven growth.


Ecosystem-Led Growth is not just a tactic, it’s a strategic evolution in how companies go to market. It combines technology, AI, trust, and partnership data to create a new model of scalable growth, one where success is multiplied through collaboration rather than confined by competition. Crossbeam provides the infrastructure, intelligence, and tools that make this approach possible, empowering businesses to grow together while preserving control over their data and relationships.

## Solutions by Team


Crossbeam delivers tailored solutions for every function that plays a role in driving Ecosystem-Led Growth. Each team (whether partnerships, sales, marketing, or operations) can leverage Crossbeam to uncover opportunities, accelerate pipeline, and strengthen customer relationships. By securely connecting partner data across the organization, Crossbeam enables every department to work smarter and more collaboratively toward shared business goals.


Tech Partners use Crossbeam to power product integrations and enhance customer value through complementary solutions. By mapping overlapping customers, open opportunities and prospects with integration partners, they can identify mutual users, prioritize co-marketing campaigns, and measure adoption impact. Crossbeam helps these teams expand their ecosystems strategically, strengthen platform stickiness, and uncover net-new opportunities for joint innovation and upsell.


Channel Partners rely on Crossbeam to optimize their reseller and distributor relationships. The platform allows them to match customer and prospect data with their indirect sales networks, revealing where channel partners can drive growth through co-selling and joint pipeline execution. With visibility into shared accounts and opportunity overlaps, channel teams can coordinate efforts more effectively, align incentives, and close deals faster through trusted, data-backed collaboration.


For Sales teams, Crossbeam transforms ecosystem data into actionable intelligence directly within their workflow. Sellers can see which prospects already have relationships with partners, access warm introductions, and collaborate with partner account executives to accelerate deal velocity. The platform’s integrations with CRMs like Salesforce ensure that these insights are visible where sellers work every day, helping them prioritize high-potential accounts and increase win rates through partner influence.


Marketing teams leverage Crossbeam to execute ecosystem-driven campaigns that target high-value prospects validated through partner overlaps. By aligning on shared audiences and combining data sets, marketers can co-create campaigns, run joint events, and measure partner-influenced pipeline. This approach enhances targeting precision, improves conversion rates, and strengthens brand credibility by reaching customers through trusted partner channels.


Revenue Operations (RevOps) teams use Crossbeam to centralize partnership data and align it with the company’s broader go-to-market systems. By connecting ecosystem insights with CRM and BI tools, they can analyze the impact of partnerships on revenue performance, track partner influence, and optimize attribution models. RevOps plays a key role in operationalizing partner data, ensuring that ecosystem intelligence is accessible, measurable, and embedded across sales and marketing processes.


For Customer Success teams, Crossbeam provides a clear view of shared customers across the partner ecosystem. This visibility helps identify opportunities for co-retention, upsell, and product adoption by coordinating with partners serving the same accounts. Customer Success can proactively collaborate with partner teams to deliver better customer outcomes, strengthen relationships, and reduce churn through a unified, ecosystem-based approach.


Finally, Crossbeam also supports Lead Generation by uncovering new prospects. By identifying companies that match your ideal customer profile but aren’t yet in your CRM, Crossbeam enables warm, trust-based outreach that increases engagement and conversion rates. By using partner data to guide prospecting, Crossbeam helps you focus on the right leads and turn ecosystem insights into real pipeline growth.
